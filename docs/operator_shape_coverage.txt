Representative core shapes: 20 per operator
===========================================

Scope: MQA, Swin, Adaptive Span, and MoE only. GQA and unrelated core sections
are byte-for-byte unchanged by this selection. This replaces the earlier
33/41/32/61-shape candidate matrix; it does not append more cases.

Each operator has exactly 20 parameter sets (80 total). MQA masked/unmasked
and MoE padded/grouped references reuse the same respective 20 shapes.
FP16/BF16/FP32 and alternate references expand measurements, not shape count.
MoE has 10 rank-2 plus 10 rank-3 inputs; Swin has 10 rank-3 plus 10 rank-4.
MQA and Adaptive Q/K/V are rank 4 by their public contracts.
Correctness tests retain their independent boundary, stride, and error checks.

Selection principles
--------------------
Keep realistic decode/prefill dimensions and several representative tail cases.
Keep the largest useful stress cases; do not remove them merely for runtime.
MoE full experts reach 16384 tokens, C=7168, E=256, F=2048, K=8;
its largest F=14336 belongs to the separate Mixtral-size family.
The 65536-token identity case measures routing only and is labeled accordingly.
The largest expert weights occupy 21 GiB at FP16/BF16 or 42 GiB at FP32,
before activations and scratch buffers. All seven expert modes remain covered.

Model families borrow dimensions rather than reproduce complete models.
Shared experts are excluded. DeepSeek-V3 dimension cases retain this operator's
normalized top-k softmax, rather than its sigmoid/group-limited router.

Parameter notation
------------------
MQA: [B,Hq,Hkv,Lq,Lk,D].
Swin: [windows,WH,WW,C,H] or [B,nW,WH,WW,C,H,optional_mask_kind].
Adaptive: [B,H,Lq,Lk,D,optional_max_span,span,ramp,span_layout,mask_kind].
  Missing options mean max_span=Lk, span=Lk/2, ramp=32, span[H], no mask.
  span_layout=0 means [H], 1 means [B,H]; mask_kind 0/1/2=none/bool/additive.
MoE: [B_or_0,S,C,E,F,K,capacity_factor,activation_id,optional_route_pattern].
  B=0 means [S,C], otherwise [B,S,C]. null capacity means no dropping.
  Activations 0..6: identity,linear,relu,gelu,silu,swiglu,geglu.
  Route pattern 0/default=random projection, 1=concentrated experts.
  F is unused by identity and is C for linear.

Validation and timing
---------------------
PPU-ZW810E: all selected 300 dtype/scenario cases passed numerical preflight.
They produced 360 successful reference comparisons with warmup=10 ms,
repetition=50 ms, median timing, using triton.testing.do_bench.
Attention uses the existing test tolerances: FP16 0.002, BF16 0.015, FP32 2e-5.
MoE uses FP16 0.004, BF16 0.03, FP32 5e-5 (absolute and relative).
On this PPU SDK, strict native FP32 required PPU_FP32_TENSOR_OVERRIDE=0.
The default large-matrix tensor path differed from CPU double by about 2.9e-4;
with that override disabled the sampled error was about 1.8e-5. The final
measurements use the override for both implementations and keep all original
error tolerances. This environment setting is specific to the validated PPU SDK.
Gate logits are checked numerically; top-k IDs remain exact. When native
rounding changes a near-tied selection, capacity/expert/combine are additionally
checked against a reference routed from the actual returned logits. Timed
references always compute their own gate projection; overrides are untimed.
Per-record validation modes and near-tie counts are included in the CSV.
The MoE int64 address fix separately passed 203 correctness tests, including
six regressions with expert strides above 2**31 elements. Swin passed 122 cases.

Geometric mean speedups, 20 parameter sets per row (native / FlagGems):
adaptive_attention_span [native]: FP16 15.332x; BF16 15.350x; FP32 6.048x
moe_gate_top_k_routing [grouped]: FP16 1.337x; BF16 1.290x; FP32 2.157x
moe_gate_top_k_routing [padded]: FP16 1.009x; BF16 1.012x; FP32 2.040x
multi_query_attention_mqa [native]: FP16 0.539x; BF16 0.538x; FP32 1.174x
multi_query_attention_mqa_mask [native]: FP16 3.347x; BF16 3.367x; FP32 1.043x
swin_transformer_attention [native]: FP16 3.081x; BF16 3.116x; FP32 1.589x

These are PPU measurements against the stated PyTorch implementations, not
optimized serving-engine or MetaX results. The MoE aggregate mixes expert
modes and is not a single-model score. Use individual cases for tuning.
The padded baseline performs extra work on unused capacity; the compact
grouped baseline is retained for comparison. Its alignment padding is timed.
Native grouped GEMM is optional and checked separately for each dtype/backend;
unsupported backends report a skip. The Triton implementation does not depend
on native grouped GEMM. No large case is filtered by elapsed time.
